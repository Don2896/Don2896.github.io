Quantitative investment · six-block pipeline

Factor-Driven Portfolio Construction

Python Factor models Monte Carlo VaR Bayesian rebalancing

A complete quantitative investment system, not a single technique in isolation: six analytical blocks run end to end on a real ten-asset portfolio (NVDA, TSLA, PLTR, GOOGL, MSFT, JPM, JNJ, XOM, GLD, SPY) across 817 trading days, 2020–2023. The thesis under test — that an AI/growth overweight beats a diversified book — is examined with evidence rather than assumed.

A three-factor regression (market, value/growth spread, oil) decomposes each asset's return before Modern Portfolio Theory optimisation, 10,000-path Monte Carlo simulation, historical-vs-analytical VaR comparison, and a rolling-window Bayesian-style rebalancing step that lets allocations respond to regime change. Every assumption violation is documented, not glossed over — all ten assets reject normality at p = 0, and the pipeline proceeds with that limitation stated plainly rather than hidden in a footnote.

→ Max-Sharpe optimisation reached a 1.382 Sharpe ratio against 0.846 equal-weight; the 34-percentage-point gap between historical (57.70%) and analytical (23.35%) 99% VaR quantifies the real cost of assuming normal returns; rolling rebalancing rotated ExxonMobil from 60% to 0% allocation as the energy supercycle reversed.

Financial risk · unsupervised ML

Fraud Detection in Bank Transactions

Python scikit-learn PCA DBSCAN

An unsupervised pipeline for surfacing fraudulent bank transactions without labelled data — built on 2,512 simulated transaction records across 495 accounts and 43 locations. Rule-based flags for suspicious timing, location, and amount feed a PCA decomposition and K-Means / DBSCAN clustering to separate genuine anomalies from noise.

A Euclidean-distance risk score in PCA space ranks transactions by how extreme their combined anomalies are — catching high-risk cases that simple flag-counting missed, since some of the highest-scoring transactions carried only a single rule-based flag.

→ 2,378 of 2,512 transactions were flagged as suspicious by the rule layer; three distinct behavioural clusters and a continuous risk score narrowed that pool to the cases worth investigating first.

Portfolio risk · diversification

Bitcoin & Ethereum: Volatility, Correlation & Diversification

Python pandas Log returns Rolling stats

Framed as a decision memo for a client weighing crypto exposure: does holding both BTC and ETH actually diversify a portfolio, or are they the same bet twice? Daily GBP-denominated closing prices were converted to log returns to keep volatility and correlation measures stationary and comparable.

30-day rolling volatility and correlation were computed across the full history, then re-examined specifically during high-volatility stress regimes — the periods where diversification actually matters.

→ BTC–ETH correlation sits above 0.8 in normal conditions and climbs toward ~0.9 during stress — the two assets behave as one correlated risk position, not independent diversifiers.

Sports analytics · exploratory

Premier League Player Performance

Python seaborn xG / xA

An exploratory analysis of Premier League output by position, using expected-goals and expected-assists as an efficiency lens rather than raw totals. Passing accuracy and volume were examined separately to separate chance creation from possession control — the two roles get judged on different metrics for good reason.

The framing throughout is recruitment-relevant: a striker scoring below xG may be riding chance volume rather than finishing quality, which matters more the more you're paying for the shirt.

→ 7 of the league's top 10 scorers outperformed their xG; every one of the top 10 assist providers outperformed xA — Son Heung-min and Harry Kane stood out as the most efficient overperformers.

Sports analytics · visualisation

Haaland — Shot Analysis, 2022/23

Python mplsoccer matplotlib

Shot-level event data from Understat is used to map every Erling Haaland shot from his debut Premier League season, sized by xG and coloured by outcome, to test a hypothesis: that Manchester City's system narrowed his shot profile into a tighter, higher-quality zone than his earlier career.

The point of the project was less the football and more the workflow — cleaning coordinate data, scaling it to a pitch reference frame, and building a visualisation that makes a spatial pattern legible at a glance rather than burying it in a table.

→ Shots cluster tightly between the goalposts and the edge of the box; high-xG chances concentrate in that same zone, and goals outpaced xG — evidence of a penalty-box role and efficient finishing.
